03 // Deal Intelligence

COUNTERPARTY & DEAL-LEVEL INTELLIGENCE

// WHO THIS IS FOR

Due diligence that goes past the data room - verifying who you're actually doing business with before capital moves.

For dealmakers where standard due diligence - legal review, financial audits, sanctions screening - is the floor and not the ceiling: it confirms what a counterparty is willing to show, not what they are not.

WHAT WE BUILD AND OPERATE.

Counterparty Vetting
Background, network, and reputational vetting on the individuals and entities behind a deal. Every significant business relationship - mergers, joint ventures, supplier agreements, investor relationships, licensing deals - connects an organization to another entity whose conduct, affiliations, and financial health it cannot fully control.
Deal-Level Due Diligence
Risk findings mapped directly to deal terms and structure, not a generic background report. It goes past the data room, verifying who you're actually doing business with before capital moves.
Undisclosed Risk Detection
Entity and relationship mapping across provided materials and the public record to surface potential undisclosed risks. A supplier that passes standard compliance screening may still maintain undisclosed relationships with sanctioned intermediaries, and a prospective investor may use beneficial ownership structures designed to obscure identity - this is built to surface exactly that gap.
Financial Investigations
Tracing financial relationships and exposure tied to a counterparty or transaction. This closes the gap between the information a decision-maker has and the information a decision actually needs.
