WHAT A DASHBOARD CAN INCLUDE.

Geographic threat map with custom layers
A map view of exposure, built with the location and layer set relevant to your operation.
Regional stability scoring
A scored view of regional instability across the geographies you operate in.
Curated signal feed
A reviewed stream of intelligence signals, not a raw, unfiltered news firehose.
Narrative & mention monitoring
Tracking of narratives and mentions tied to a principal, institution, or deal.
Principal/executive exposure alerts
Alerts tied to the specific exposure of a named principal or executive.
Travel & event advance
Threat context for destinations and events on the calendar before travel happens.
Markets, commodities & supply-chain chokepoints
Visibility into the market, commodity, and chokepoint signals that affect operations.
Infrastructure dependencies (cables, pipelines, ports)
Mapping of the physical infrastructure a facility or operation actually depends on.
Sanctions & counterparty watchlists
Screening views tied to the entities and individuals behind a relationship or deal.
Live camera views of locations of interest
Camera views of the sites, routes, and assets you need eyes on.
Analyst briefs & daily summaries
Analyst-reviewed written briefs summarizing the picture for a given day or period.
Custom alerts by keyword, region, or entity
Alerting configured around the specific keywords, regions, or entities that matter to you.
