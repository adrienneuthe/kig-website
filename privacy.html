PRIVACY POLICY.

What we collect through this site, why, and how to request deletion.

Last updated: 2026

What We Collect

This site collects information only when you choose to provide it:

  • Contact and inquiry forms (homepage, service pages, /contact, and the short form at the bottom of every page): name, email, organization (optional), type of inquiry, and your message.
  • Newsletter signup (/newsletter and the inline signup on Insights articles): your email address.
  • Site analytics: standard, aggregate usage data (pages visited, general location, device type) via Google Analytics.

We do not use visible CAPTCHA challenges; a hidden anti-spam field is used instead. We do not request payment information, government ID, or sensitive personal data through this site.

How We Use It

Form and email submissions are used only to respond to your inquiry and, if you subscribe, to send occasional briefings. Analytics data is used in aggregate to understand site usage and improve the site. We do not sell contact form or newsletter data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Service Providers

To operate this site, submitted information passes through a small number of processors acting on our behalf, under their own security and privacy practices: our form-delivery provider (Formspree) routes inquiry submissions to our inbox; our email provider (ActiveCampaign) sends newsletter briefings to subscribers; and Google Analytics (GA4) processes aggregate site-usage data. These providers do not use your information for their own purposes, and we do not otherwise sell or share your contact form or newsletter data with third parties.

Confidentiality

Inquiries submitted through this site are treated as confidential and are reviewed only by the KIG team member(s) needed to respond.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from briefings at any time using the link in any email. To request deletion of information you've submitted through this site, email project@kronusintelligencegroup.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy: project@kronusintelligencegroup.com.
